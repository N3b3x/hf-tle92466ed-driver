uint8_t tle92466ed::CurrentFeedbackBatch::rejected_chains {0}

Read chains discarded because their reply anchors did not verify.

Non-zero means the bus mis-ordered replies and the affected channels were reported as "no window" rather than decoded from another channel's data. A rising count is a bus-integrity signal, not a plant fault.
