◆ min_int_thresh

uint8_t tle92466ed::ChannelConfig::min_int_thresh {CH_CTRL_REG::MIN_INT_THRESH_FLOOR}

Minimum ICC integrator threshold (CTRL.MIN_INT_THRESH). Defaults to the datasheet §4.6.2.4 floor rather than the POR value of 0: at 0 the effective threshold can go negative and the power stage stops switching while the channel still reports enabled with its setpoint intact.
